# Training modal accordion

## Changes
- Replace the separately visible Videos and Documents lists with one accordion; only one section can be open at a time.
- Keep Videos open by default and Documents collapsed.
- Display video cards in a compact two-column grid, sized to accommodate two additional videos.
- Remove the training modal's internal scrolling and keep its opt-out and action row visible.
- Preserve the existing video player and PDF-opening behavior.
